# First Steps

Welcome! This quickstart gets you up and running with Ultra Tasks: you will try the web app, add and manage tasks, use the time tracker, then see how to install the desktop app and where to go next (sync, backups, and other resources).

---

## Part 1: Quick Tour (web app)

Open your installed Ultra Tasks app, or run a local web build using [[2.11-Run-the-Development-Server]]. On a new install, a short setup offers **Start fresh**, **Restore backup**, or **Import tasks**, and lets you choose an appearance. Restore and import open the existing import settings so you can choose a file; they do not replace data just by selecting the option. You can skip setup and change the appearance later in **Settings → Appearance**. The short “Getting started” example task is added once setup closes, and not at all if you chose restore or import.

A few short tips guide you to your first task and explain where tasks live: **Today** shows what's planned for today, and the **Inbox** keeps every task that isn't in a project. The last tip offers **Show me more**: a quick tour on your own task (time tracking, subtasks, projects and tags, Upcoming) that ends with what else you can switch on in **Settings → General → App features**. It stays under **Help → Quick tour**, and guided tours are under **Help → How to**. Coming from Todoist or TickTick? See [[2.20-Import-from-Todoist]] and [[2.22-Import-from-TickTick]].

[[assets/1.01-First-Steps-first-run.png]]

**Add a task:** Use the "+" button in the top right or `Shift+A`. Add as many tasks as you like. Short-hand syntax is available (similar to many other task managers). Press Escape or click outside the task overlay when done.

[[assets/1.01-First-Steps-create-task.png]]

**Time tracking:** Ultra Tasks integrates time tracking as a core feature. You can remove or add time usage later. Start and stop the timer from anywhere in the app with the play button in the header.

[[assets/1.01-First-Steps-start-time-tracking.png]]

**Task details:** Open the side panel on any task to edit its details: hover over the task and click the panel icon on its right, or select the task and press `I`. On a touch screen, tap the task.

[[assets/1.01-First-Steps-task-details.png]]

**Delete a task:** Right-click the task and use "Delete task" at the bottom, or select the task and press Backspace.

[[assets/1.01-First-Steps-delete-task.png]]

Next, open **Settings** (at the bottom of the side menu).

The web app is limited to your browser on one computer at first. If Ultra Tasks works for you, you will likely want to configure **Sync** so your data is saved reliably—even on a single computer. We can skip Sync for now and set it up later (see [[1.02-Configure-Data-Synchronization]]).

**Finish day (optional):** Finish day is switched off on a new install. Turn it on under **Settings → General → App features**, and the main Today view shows a "Finish day" button below your tasks. It moves completed tasks to the archive and shows an end-of-day report: time tracked vs estimates, then subjective ratings (how you felt while working). Without it, the list of done tasks offers **Move completed tasks to archive** instead.

[[assets/1.01-First-Steps-finish-day-0.png]]

[[assets/1.01-First-Steps-finish-day-1.png]]

That's the basics. Ultra Tasks supports many working styles; focus on adding tasks and using the time tracker for now. You can explore more options when you're ready.

## Part 3: Additional Resources and next Steps

**If you make a mistake:** You can restore from a backup. See [[2.02-Restore-Data-From-Backup]].

**External getting-started material:**

Ultra Tasks is based on Super Productivity, so the upstream material below still explains the core ideas. It was written for Super Productivity, and some screens and labels differ in Ultra Tasks.

- [Getting started with Super Productivity](https://dev.to/johannesjo/getting-started-with-super-productivity-2791) (upstream article, dev.to) — recommended first read.
- [Super Productivity Introduction](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VoF2_RSdNXA) (upstream video, YouTube) — longer walkthrough.
- [Ultra Tasks issues](https://github.com/ac9492/ultra-tasks/issues) — ask questions, report problems, and suggest ideas for Ultra Tasks.

Other upstream articles: [Super Productivity: How to grow fond of time tracking and task management](https://dev.to/johannesjo/super-productivity-how-to-grow-fond-of-time-tracking-and-task-management-22ee), [The Prioritising Scheme – How to "eat the frog" with Super Productivity](https://dev.to/johannesjo/the-prioritising-scheme-how-to-eat-the-frog-with-super-productivity-mlk).

In the app, **Help** lists the **Quick tour**, the guided **How to** tours, and links for getting help and reporting problems.
