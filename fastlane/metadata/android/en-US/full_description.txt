Ultra Tasks is a calm, free and open-source to-do list, planner and time tracker. It works offline, needs no account, and has no ads or tracking in the app.

<b>Capture and plan</b>
• Add tasks in a few keystrokes with projects, tags, estimates and scheduling shorthand
• Break bigger tasks into subtasks; keep quick thoughts in the Inbox
• See the week ahead in Upcoming and give work a time slot in the calendar
• Reminders, a home screen widget and a focus mode

<b>Track your time</b>
• Start and stop a timer on any task, with estimates and a daily summary
• Pomodoro-style focus sessions

<b>Your data stays yours</b>
• Tasks, notes and settings are stored on your device
• Optional sync (WebDAV, OneDrive, self-hosted UltraSync) and optional integrations such as calendars and issue trackers; they only contact the services you configure
• Import from Super Productivity, Todoist and TickTick
• Open source under AGPL-3.0; the F-Droid build contains no Google Play services

Ultra Tasks is a fork of Super Productivity with its own app ID (app.ultratasks), so it installs alongside the original.
